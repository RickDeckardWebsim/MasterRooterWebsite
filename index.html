What I Handle

The core jobs I handle, day in and day out.

  1. 01

    Main Line Sewer Cleaning

    The main line is the pipe carrying everything from your house out to the street. When it backs up, drains gurgle, toilets won't flush right, and water can back up into tubs or floor drains. I run a motorized cable through the line to cut through roots, grease, and buildup and get it flowing again.

  2. 02

    Small Line Drain Clearing

    A slow-draining sink, tub, or shower usually means a clog in that one branch line, not the main sewer. I clear these smaller lines directly, without the heavier equipment a main line job needs.

  3. 03

    Sewer Locating & Camera Location

    Before anyone digs up a yard or driveway, I run a small camera down the line to see exactly what's going on and mark the problem spot on the surface. It saves guesswork, time, and unnecessary digging.

  4. 04

    Toilet & Stool Clog Service

    Some toilet clogs won't budge no matter how much plunging you do. I pull the toilet if needed, clear the blockage directly, and reset it so it's sealed and flushing properly.

  5. 05

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance

    Tree roots and grease buildup cause the same problems again and again if nothing's done between emergencies. A cleaning on a regular schedule catches that buildup early, before it turns into a backed-up house on a Sunday night.
